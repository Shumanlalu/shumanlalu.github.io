Built for real conversations

Messaging that respects the people using it.

NEXCHAT keeps encryption, reliability and operator control in one Android app. No public sign-up is offered on this website.

⌁

Signal encryption

One-to-one and group messages use per-device Signal sessions. Group delivery is encrypted separately for every member device.

◇

Offline-first queue

Composed messages are stored in an encrypted local outbox and retried when the server becomes reachable.

◎

Identity checks

Numeric safety numbers, QR display and verification markers help detect identity changes.
