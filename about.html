About NEXCHAT

Encryption first. Metadata honest.

NEXCHAT is a self-hostable Android messaging application. Message content is encrypted with the Signal protocol before it leaves a device, and the server relays opaque envelopes rather than readable conversations.

⌁

What the server can see

The relay necessarily handles routing metadata such as sender and recipient device identifiers, delivery timing and group identifiers. It does not receive message plaintext.

Operators can still delay, withhold or observe metadata. Safety numbers should be compared over a separate trusted channel.

◇

What stays on the device

Private keys, the SQLCipher database passphrase and session tokens are protected using Android Keystore material. Signing out wipes the local encrypted database and device identity.

Design principles

No custom cryptography.

NEXCHAT reuses the Signal protocol for one-to-one and group content. It does not invent its own cipher. Attachments and call signaling also travel inside encrypted content where applicable.

Remote configuration, announcements and account restrictions are controlled by the private operator panel and enforced by the backend.

01Online messaging

Authenticated WebSocket with encrypted envelope relay and long-poll fallback.

02Private groups

Pairwise encrypted fan-out to each member device, with membership checks on the server.

03Nearby relay

Bluetooth Classic transport with encrypted routing headers. Real-device verification is still required.

04Local resilience

Durable encrypted outbox, receipts, deduplication and reconnect synchronisation.
